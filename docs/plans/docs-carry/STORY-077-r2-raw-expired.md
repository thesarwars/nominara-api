# STORY-077 R2 - `raw_expired` on GET /v1/results/{id} (owner decision 100(f), 2026-11-12), to carry to the docs repo

**api-specification.md section 6 and the route table row for `GET /results/{id}`:** replace "`507 retention_exceeded` past the plan's raw-capture retention" with: past the plan's raw-capture retention (or once the retention sweep has marked the capture, `payload.raw_expired`), the route answers **200** with the capture's facts (entities, citations, metrics) and its retained excerpt, **no raw text**, and `raw_expired: true`. Then `raw_response.text` is `""`, `raw_response.char_count` and `capture.response_chars` are `0` (meaning "not available", not "an empty answer"), `highlight_map` is `[]`, and `raw_response.excerpt` carries `answer_captures.response_excerpt` (null if none). Inside the window `raw_expired` is `false` and `excerpt` is absent. The boundary is the one the sweep and `/raw` use (`rawRetentionCutoff`, including a slice-B deferral). A cell that failed or was blocked is still `422 precondition_unmet`. **`GET /results/{id}/raw`, `POST /results/{id}/reparse` and the list are unchanged: still `507 retention_exceeded`.**

**STORY-034 AC10:** the "result document past retention is 507" clause is amended the same way; tests `034-AC10d` and `034-AC10e` in `test/integration/tracking-results-reparse.spec.ts` were rewritten (they pinned the old 507 on this route), and boundary, facts-kept, tenant and failed-cell tests were added.

**Fix round (audit):** a reused (L2) result is `raw_expired` when its SOURCE is past the cutoff or marked expired (the source's text is never served through an in-window reused row). When `raw_expired`, `entities[].occurrences` is `[]` and `sentiment.evidence_char_*` are null (the same withholding as a stale normaliser; the contract keeps `char_start/char_end` non-null, so occurrences are omitted rather than nulled), and `raw_response.normalized_text_offsets` is `false` (the schema is now `boolean`, was `literal(true)`). `/raw` and `/reparse` had no L2 text path (a reused row has no object and no text of its own: 404 and 422) and are pinned by tests.
